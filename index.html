Our story

Pressed by hand, kept for years

Petals & Pages is a small homegrown studio in Matale, born from a love of fresh flowers and the books that deserve beautiful company.

Every piece is made slowly: flowers are pressed, arranged, and sealed by hand. The result is a little keepsake that carries nature's softness into your everyday pages.
